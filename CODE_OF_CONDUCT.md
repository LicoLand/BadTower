# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants in the BadTower project are expected to keep collaboration
technical, respectful, and harassment-free, regardless of background or
identity.

## Expected behavior

- Focus on technical merit and evidence.
- Assume good faith and give constructive feedback.
- Respect the repository boundary and review outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, secrets, or runtime data.
- Bad-faith disruption of review, testing, or release processes.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to all project spaces, including the issue tracker, pull
requests, and release channels of <https://github.com/LicoLand/BadTower>.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository advisory
channel at <https://github.com/LicoLand/BadTower/security/advisories>.
Maintainers review reports and may remove content, reject contributions, or
restrict participation as needed.
